How it works in the real app

Typing
Suggestions come from an Indian-language transliteration model (AI4Bharat IndicXlit) running on the state's own servers, plus the state's list of names, places and legal words.
Speaking
Speech becomes text in the speaker's language. The audio is stored with the text, and the officer reads it back before anyone signs.
Translating
Translations are always marked as machine-made and never replace the original words in a statement or FIR.
