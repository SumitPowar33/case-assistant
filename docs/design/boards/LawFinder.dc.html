Which law applies?

  • Offence committed before 1 July 2024: charge under the IPC.
  • Offence on or after 1 July 2024: charge under the BNS.
  • Procedure and evidence: the new codes (BNSS, BSA) for cases registered from 1 July 2024. If unsure for an older case, ask the prosecutor.
