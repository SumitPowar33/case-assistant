# Official forms the app must produce (research notes, 22 Oct 2026)

All from public government sources. Field lists are the starting point for data models; the state pack can rename or add fields.

## 1. FIR – CCTNS form IIF-1 (CAS 4.5, Delhi Police training manual)
Ten tabs, mandatory fields marked in CAS:
1. Acts & sections (needs a General Diary entry first)
2. Occurrence – date/time from–to, "known/unknown"; place
3. Complainant – personal details, address
4. FIR contents – the complaint text (tahrir)
5. Victim – known/unknown; personal, address, other info, statement
6. Accused – known/unknown; personal, address, other info (occupation, nationality, passport, ID), physical description
7. Property of interest – category, details (vehicle no., colour, value)
8. Hurt case details
9. Action taken (mandatory) – investigate/assign IO, refused (section + reason), self-investigation
10. Signature
Zero FIR: 9 tabs; occurrence = place + "outside limits"; action defaults to "Transferred".
Other CCTNS registration forms: Complaint, NCR, MLC, Missing person, Lost property, Unidentified dead body/unnatural death, Preventive action, Unclaimed property.

Related CCTNS forms: IIF-2 crime details, IIF-3 arrest, IIF-4 property seizure, IIF-5 final report (chargesheet), IIF-6 court disposal, IIF-7 result of appeal (UP Police circular, Apr 2025).

## 2. BNSS formats (Delhi Police compilation, 2024)
| Form | Key fields |
|---|---|
| Notice for appearance, BNSS 35(3) | FIR/case no., sections, PS, diary no.; noticee name, address, phone/email; date/time to appear; conditions (a)–(j); warning under 35(6); IO name, designation, seal |
| Arrest memo, BNSS 35 | name, alias, parentage; contacts; present/permanent address; FIR/DD no. and sections; place/date/time of arrest; person informed; arresting officer; grounds (a)–(e); arrestee signature; witnesses |
| Notice to produce document, BNSS 94 | complaint details; addressee; list of documents; production date/time at PS; IO; PS; date |
| Notice to witness, BNSS 179 | witness details; date/time/PS; exemption note (under 15, over 60, women, disabled, acutely ill) |
| Seizure memo of documents | FIR, sections, PS; person producing; table (S.No., description, pages); two witnesses; IO |
| Personal search memo, BNSS 49 | person; articles recovered; two witnesses; IO |
| Request for medical examination | FIR/DD; patient details; custodial constables; IO |
| FSL forwarding letter | FIR, sections; brief facts; exhibits table (description, source, marking, sheets); examination needed; points for opinion |
| Progress of investigation, BNSS 193(3)(ii) | FIR, sections; addressee; progress points; IO contact |
| Certificate, BSA 63(4)(c) | Part A (party) and Part B (expert): device type, make/model/serial, IMEI/MAC/cloud ID; ownership/control; hash values (SHA1/SHA256/MD5); name, signature, date, time (IST), place |

## 3. Chargesheet – BNSS 193 (contents, not a fixed form; the state prescribes the form)
- Names of parties; nature of information; names of persons acquainted with the circumstances
- Whether an offence appears committed and by whom; arrest / bail / custody status
- Victim's medical report for BNS 64–71
- Sequence of custody for electronic devices – 193(3)(i)
- Documents and statements relied on (incl. BNSS 180 statements) – 193(6); request to exclude parts – 193(7)
- Indexed copies for the accused, electronic service allowed – 193(8)
- Supplementary report for further evidence – 193(9)
- Deadlines: 60/90 days from arrest (BNSS 187(3)); 2 months from FIR for BNS 64–71 and POCSO 4, 6, 8, 10 (193(2)); victim update within 90 days (193(3)(ii))

## 4. Case diary – BNSS 192
Daily entries: time information reached, time investigation began and closed, places visited, circumstances ascertained; statements recorded under BNSS 180. Bound volume, paginated. Court may use it as an aid; accused generally cannot see it.
